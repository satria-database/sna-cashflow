# Terrava Project Guidelines & Design System Rules

## Strict UI/UX Theme Directive: Green & White (Emerald & White)
- **Primary Color Palette**: Emerald Green (`#007a52`, `bg-emerald-600`, `bg-emerald-700`, `text-emerald-700`, `bg-emerald-50`, `border-emerald-200`) paired with crisp white (`bg-white`) and soft neutral surfaces (`bg-slate-50`, `border-slate-200`).
- **PROHIBITED - No Black/Dark Surfaces**:
  - **NEVER** use dark/black container cards, hero blocks, or dark mode widgets (e.g. `bg-slate-900`, `bg-slate-950`, `bg-black`, or deep dark green `bg-[#062c21]`).
  - **NEVER** build dark-themed widgets for settlement reports, financial metrics, summary cards, or data tables.
  - All new cards, widgets, and features must consistently use clean white backgrounds (`bg-white`), subtle emerald borders/accents, and clear dark slate text (`text-slate-900`, `text-slate-700`, `text-slate-600`) for maximum legibility and visual harmony.
- **Buttons & Interactive Controls**:
  - Primary CTA buttons must use Terrava Emerald (`bg-[#007a52] hover:bg-[#006644] text-white`).
  - Secondary/Tertiary buttons must use white or light emerald (`bg-white border-slate-200` or `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-800 border-emerald-200`).
  - Do NOT use `bg-slate-900` or black buttons.
